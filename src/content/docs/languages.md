GitLoom's interface is available in 16 languages. By default it follows your system language.

## Choose a language

Open **Settings ▸ General ▸ Language** and pick one, or *System* to follow macOS. The new language takes effect after GitLoom restarts; *Restart Now* does that for you, keeping your tabs.

## What stays in English

The names of git commands and terms (commit, branch, merge and so on) stay in English. Some messages built while GitLoom runs are in English only for now.

## The language AI writes in

Commit messages and other text written by AI have their own setting: **Settings ▸ AI ▸ Writing ▸ Language**, English or Tiếng Việt.
